Hello! Welcome back to another blog post! I really love sharing my process so here's my very first official devlog! This month's devlog will be focusing on my process of BLOX FABLES' big refactor, as well as sneak peaks of Chapter 3! Hope y'all enjoy!

THE REFACTOR

I know the Refactor doesn't change much for viewers, aside from the few visual adjustments I added for funsies, but oh my GOD it makes working with the site much easier.

I coded the original site in a frenzy. I was struggling to get it off the ground and settle on a look, and then just… did all of it in one night. Since then there's been some small touch ups here and there, and I'd always add them in a sort of rush which led to my code not looking the best nor being easy to add onto.

Eventually I took a small break to work on my personal (this place!) and I learned A LOT. So much so that it made working on Blox Fables suck so bad JSHDJNA. So I've been redoing things! I also got a few funny bugs while messing with things.

I think this one was my favorite:

This took a lot of time but it's sooo worth it. It makes updating Charred Pizza a lot easier, as well as reusing scripts in external files so I don't have to fucking. Copy paste the same script element every time. Hoping to have it up within the next few weeks or so! Here's a sneak peek from the refactor!

CHAPTER 3'S PROGRESS

This is the fun one. Chapter 3's progress has been slow. I'm gonna have to cut a few of the secrets I was excited for. I definitely bit off more than I could chew, and as a result it made me incredibly unmotivated to work on the chapter.

It didn't help I had a lot of trouble with Nil's character voice. It was so un-fun. It was excruciating to write, and I was struggling to figure out how to pick up from Chapter 2. I had everything I wanted in the chapter planned out, I just couldn't figure out a fun start!

Thankfully, due to a great recommendation from Tato, I finally found his voice! The novel "Klara and the Sun" by Kazuo Ishiguro has been a huge help! Now he's just as fun to write as the others, and I've been making great progress! As of writing this devlog, there's around 1500 words in Chapter 3.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Here's the fun bits. I originally had a lot more planned for Chapter 3, but as mentioned before— had to cut it. I am aiming for at least 2 secrets though, one of course counting the usual type of secret found in every chapter.

I also finally settled on a Chapter Name! I don't personally like it as much as the previous ones, so it might change, but currently the title is "BLIND FAITH." Make of that what you will. Below is also a preview of the current chapter!

And just for fun and for all of y'all being so so patient with this long wait, here's some thumbnails for the header:

Thanks again for all your patience for the wait on both Blox Fables and Charred Pizza. It means a lot to both me and Tato. I'm so excited for y'all to see the refactor and chapter 3, I've got some fun characters for y'all in the upcoming chapters as well as maybe a secret for the refactor…

THE TRIP TO OPEN SAUCE

SEPTEMBER 5, 2026

Welcome to my first blog post!!! This trip actually took place in mid-July and goddd so much happened it was a blast! I got to see both my partners in person AND spend an entire weekend with them, play Animation Versus early, meet the team behind the Animator vs. Animation series, and so much other cool stuff at Open Sauce!

The first thing I want to go over is the TCG project Schaf, I, and Storgic hosted. We each had different roles. Schaf organized and created the server as well as ordered the cards for both the artists who wanted them and the AVA/M team, I was in charge of templates and putting together the cards, and Storge got the people! We wanted to do a large deck of TCG-styled cards of different Animator vs. Animation characters. Each fan-artist would take a different characters, and it went crazy! People went fucking crazy with the art in these cards!

Unfortunatly, we didn't have an estimation for shipping and sure enough, they didn't get there on time, BUT. I had a backup plan. The day before I left, I bought a whole lot of bullshit and spent the entire day printing out the cards myself, glueing the fronts and backs together, laminating and cutting them. It was not easy, but by the end, it was all done! I even made extra copies of mine's, Schaf's, and Tato's for us to get signed!

The travel was definitely exhausting. To make this trip possible, I had to go cheap as possible for the flight, but I'm glad I could afford it. I spent both Friday and Monday almost entirely on flights and airports. I prefer to do carry-on-- I pack the minimum so I can bring both my bag and suitcase on the plane with me, I've heard enough nightmares about baggage claim to terrify me into using it as little as possible. Unfortunately everybody else had the same idea and I had to give up my luggage. Twice! Was an anxious mess the entire time because I was so scared of my two favorite shirts (my shirt of King from AVM, and another fanmade one of Magolor from Kirby) being lost for good. Espescially the Magolor one-- it was a 24hr drop, I can't get it back!

Thankfully, there was no issue. At the airport, I met my partner Schaf, who was picking me up-- and she has a website too! Check it out here. It was so fucking amazing seeing them in person-- and they are a goddamn ANGEL for picking me up and driving through the hell that is San Francisco roads. If you and Tato are reading this just know I love y'all so much!

We made it to the hotel, and I need to say there was NOTHING good on. We tried settling for Family Guy, but it was just a boring episode-- and we stumbled upon what we thought was a cooking show. I don't remember the name of it, but it was edited like a painfully outdated Youtube video and they weren't even cooking! They were reacting to obviously edited cooking-related shitposts and fails! Like how the hell did that make it to cable?? It was so fucking bizarre. We ended up just going to bed. I still find it hilarious just how weird that was.

The real fun started Saturday morning when we met with Tato and Schaf. We met Tato outside the hotel and decided to uber to the convention. Schaf was an angel yet again by paying for the "I hate waiting" upgrades-- so we got to skip the entire line which was backed up to hell and back. The passes were also so cool! They were made of PCB and even had a simple tutorial on the back of them! You could also get them signed, but I wanted to focus on getting my handmade card of King signed.

The event was fucking awesome. We met some other AVAM fans, and even met the team! They were all so fucking cool! I got to talk to Muno-- the lead engineer for the game-- who started the entire game with his Rivals of Aether Yellow mod. He is such an inspiration to me when it comes to gamedev and programming, so it felt so nice to talk to him. DJ was also incredibly cool, and I even got to play a match against the thumbnail artist Tori!

I got my card signed by the entire team: Alan Becker, DJ, Charlie, Tori, Skim, and Muno and I think it is now my favorite possession. I want to go into further detail on the game in another blog post-- as this one is already pretty long-- but oh my god. I am more than happy with the money I spent in the kickstarter and excitedly waiting for every update.

It was also surreal to spend the convention with my partners. We got to play matches against eachother-- Tato destroyed me and Schaf haha-- I still remember the ways they played and I'm so excited for when I can rematch them once the beta drops. Schaf was so skilled with TSC's grab and Tato was insanely unpredicatable it was SCARY.

I still can't believe the time I spent with them. This series brought us all together, both virtually and physically. There were so many cool booths and different things. We were even able to give the cards to the team and they even went through them one by one! Awgh!

I think my favorite booths were the cool flesh-fabric creature, the keyboard with the most used words, and obviously Animation Versus.
